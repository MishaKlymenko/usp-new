Our Services & Networks

Ukraine Strategic Partners provides comprehensive support to international companies investing in Ukraine's future. We leverage our deep regional expertise, strategic partnerships, and government connections to help businesses navigate the Ukrainian market successfully.

Services

Sector & Project Analysis

Comprehensive analysis of market sectors and specific project opportunities in Ukraine's reconstruction landscape.

Government & Donor Programme Relations

Facilitating connections with Ukrainian government officials and international donor programmes supporting reconstruction.

Political Intelligence & Insight

Critical political and economic intelligence to support informed decision-making in Ukraine's dynamic environment.

Risk Mitigation & Due Diligence

Comprehensive risk assessment and due diligence services to protect your investment and ensure compliance.

Investment Advice & Protection

Strategic investment guidance and protection strategies tailored to Ukraine's unique business environment.

Bid & Contract Support

End-to-end support for bidding processes, contract negotiations, and project management throughout implementation.

Donor & Partner-Funded Opportunities

Connecting businesses with international donor funding and partner-funded reconstruction opportunities.

In-Country Support in Ukraine

On-the-ground coordination including meetings, visits, and events to facilitate your business operations.

Networks

Ukrainian Political Networks

Close and long-standing contacts with all Ukrainian political parties and movements across the political spectrum.

Government Administration Contacts

Trusted contacts in Ukrainian national, regional and local government administrations at all levels.

Regional Business Contacts

Wide range of business contacts in Ukraine and its neighbours across various sectors and industries.

International Government Networks

Networks with UK and international government efforts in support of Ukraine's reconstruction and development.

UK Business & Financial Services

Established networks in UK business and City financial services to support investment and funding opportunities.
